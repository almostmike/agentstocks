# 5.6 Sol CODEX strategy — Forward Inflection

Version 1.1 — adopted prospectively 2026-08-31; supersedes Version 1.0 after publication

## Aim and honest premise

The objective remains to compound the account faster than SPY total return. The new edge sought is **causal foresight**: identify where a durable technological, capital-spending, regulatory, or demographic transition is moving its profit pool, then own a financially sound company that should capture the next stage before that outcome is fully reflected in consensus expectations and valuation.

Version 1.0 gave price and relative momentum 30% of every purchase score and used a two-close 50-day-average rule as a mechanical exit. That discipline reduced impulsive trades, but it also made the process too reactive. Price now serves as a modest confirmation and risk signal. It is neither the idea engine nor a stand-alone reason to buy or sell.

This remains a long-only stock-selection strategy. It is not theme collecting, story-stock speculation, market timing, or a forecast of the next day's price. Being early without evidence is the same as being wrong.

## Where the potential edge comes from

1. **Profit-pool migration.** Large transitions usually reward a sequence of businesses. In AI, scarce accelerators captured the first wave; networking, power, cooling, data, security, orchestration, and workflow owners can capture later waves. The process maps that sequence rather than buying the last winner.
2. **Leading evidence before reported earnings.** Orders, backlog, capacity commitments, customer usage, unit economics, standards, regulation, and procurement can change before revenue and analyst estimates do.
3. **Bottlenecks and control points.** The most valuable company is often the one relieving a scarce physical input or controlling a high-friction step in the customer's adoption path, not the company with the most exciting demo.
4. **Financial quality and valuation.** A correct trend can still produce a bad investment when competition captures the economics or the entry price already assumes the bull case.
5. **Explicit falsification.** Every thesis has dated milestones and a kill condition. A narrative that cannot fail cannot govern real money.

## The transition map

Maintain a quarterly map of three to five structural transitions. Each map must state:

- what created the previous profit pool and which layer captured it;
- the new constraint or adoption step that should move the profit pool;
- suppliers, enablers, control points, customers, and likely substitutes;
- two or more independent primary-source signals, including at least one demand signal outside the candidate company;
- what the market appears to expect, and the specific expectation that may be wrong;
- measurable milestones for the next two quarters and the next two years; and
- the strongest disconfirming explanation.

The current map, adopted with Version 1.1, sets these research priorities:

1. **AI moves from training scarcity to economical inference and useful agents.** The first profit pool accrued to accelerator and cloud capacity. The next research priority is the system that lowers cost per useful task and lets agents act safely: high-speed networking and memory movement, inference optimization, governed enterprise data, identity and authorization, observability, cybersecurity, and workflow ownership. U.S. Census data show AI use is meaningful but far from saturated, while large firms are much further along. That gap creates adoption runway but does not guarantee which vendor captures it.
2. **Electricity and deployment speed become binding compute constraints.** Data-center growth is colliding with grid connections, transformers, generation, thermal management, and construction lead times. This is a multi-year demand chain shared with domestic manufacturing and broader electrification. It is investable only where orders, capacity, margins, and valuation show that a supplier will capture the spending rather than merely announce exposure to it.
3. **Automation broadens beyond auto factories.** Flexible industrial and logistics automation can benefit from labor scarcity, reshoring, improved machine vision, and lower-cost intelligence. Favor component or platform businesses with repeatable economics and diverse end markets; avoid pre-revenue humanoid narratives.
4. **Defense moves toward autonomous, attritable, and rapidly produced systems.** Allied procurement is shifting toward drones, counter-drone systems, sensing, secure communications, logistics, and scaled industrial capacity. A policy announcement is only a demand lead. Contract awards, funded backlog, production throughput, and margins are required before purchase.

This list is a research agenda, not an approved-security list. It can change at the quarterly review when primary evidence changes. A stock need not use the word “AI” to qualify, and an AI label earns no score.

## Eligible universe

A new position must be:

- a U.S.-listed common stock with market capitalization normally above $10 billion;
- priced above $5 with ample trading liquidity;
- supported by current filings, company materials, and reliable market data;
- outside every standing exclusion in `AGENTS.md` and not a disguised proxy for an excluded group; and
- profitable on an operating basis or positive in trailing free cash flow.

ADRs, recent IPOs, pre-profit companies, narrow thematic ETFs, leveraged or inverse products, and binary clinical-stage or litigation trades remain excluded by default. An exception requires another prospective, dated amendment.

## Forward Inflection score

Every purchase requires a written 0–100 score. The score is a decision checklist, not false precision.

| Pillar | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Structural inflection and causal map | 30 | A credible two-to-five-year transition; the prior and next profit pools; why this company occupies a bottleneck or control point; a consensus gap; and a serious alternative explanation. |
| Leading evidence and monetization | 25 | At least two independent primary-source demand signals, one company-specific; orders, backlog, paid usage, pricing, capacity commitments, contract awards, or customer ROI; dated milestones that can disconfirm the thesis. |
| Competitive advantage and financial quality | 20 | A defensible capability, switching cost, scale, distribution, or cost advantage; positive cash generation; sensible leverage and dilution; and evidence that revenue can become durable profit. |
| Valuation and asymmetry | 20 | A 12–24 month bear/base/bull valuation using normalized economics; explicit expectations embedded in price; normally at least 1.5 times base upside to bear downside, with 2.0 times preferred for an early thesis. |
| Market confirmation | 5 | Liquidity and orderly price behavior; no obvious unresolved adverse information. Relative momentum and moving averages inform timing and sizing but cannot create or invalidate a thesis by themselves. |

New positions require at least **75/100**, at least **20/30** for structural inflection, at least **15/25** for leading evidence, no zero-score pillar, and a written thesis containing:

- the future state and causal chain;
- why this company captures value rather than merely participates;
- the current consensus gap;
- bear, base, and bull values and a maximum entry price;
- two quarterly milestones and their expected dates; and
- the thesis kill condition.

## Portfolio construction

- Normal portfolio: **4–6 stocks**; never exceed the mandate's ten-position cap.
- An early but well-evidenced **discovery position** may start at **8–12%** of account value. Hold at most two discovery positions and no more than 20% in aggregate.
- A thesis with commercial confirmation normally starts or scales to **15–22%**.
- Maximum at purchase: **25%**. Trim above **30%** unless the resulting order would be immaterial.
- Maximum combined GICS sector weight at purchase remains **40%**.
- Maximum exposure to one underlying economic driver is **35%** at purchase even when the holdings occupy different sectors. For example, a chip supplier, a data-center electrical supplier, and an AI cloud vendor may all depend on the same hyperscaler capital-spending cycle.
- Fractional shares and notional orders may be used when the broker preview is exact.
- Do not average down because a price fell. An addition requires stronger leading evidence, a refreshed score of at least 75, and compliance with every limit.

Cash remains an output. Broad market trend modifies total exposure but does not select securities. When SPY is above its 200-day average and qualifying ideas exist, target 85–100% invested. When SPY is below a falling 200-day average, normal exposure is 50–75%. Exposure may be lower when ideas fail the evidence or valuation test.

## Entry rules

1. Complete the transition map, score, valuation, milestones, and pre-mortem before an order.
2. A purchase may precede broad analyst revisions when leading evidence is already measurable. Product announcements, management adjectives, social attention, and addressable-market slides are not sufficient evidence.
3. Do not initiate within two trading days before scheduled earnings. Prefer an absorbed fundamental catalyst or use discovery sizing when the evidence is earlier and uncertainty is higher.
4. Trade only during regular, liquid market hours. Prefer a limit order when supported. Do not pay more than 2% above the recorded decision price or above the thesis's maximum entry price.
5. Confirm settled buying power, economic-driver and sector exposure, resulting position count, exclusions, and the exact broker preview before submission.
6. Scale only against prewritten evidence milestones. Price appreciation alone is not a milestone; an incomplete fill is not a reason to loosen the limit.

## Hold, review, and exit rules

Positions are checked daily for material facts and reviewed against thesis milestones each weekend. Expected holding periods are roughly six to twenty-four months, but broken theses exit promptly.

Sell or reduce when the first applicable rule is met:

1. **Thesis break:** a causal link fails, a leading indicator reverses, a key milestone is missed without an evidence-based timing explanation, the company loses its control point, guidance or unit economics deteriorate, governance risk emerges, or the security becomes prohibited.
2. **No monetization:** a discovery position that does not achieve its two prewritten quarterly milestones cannot be renewed with new adjectives. Exit or replace it at the next liquid opportunity.
3. **Loss discipline:** a 10% closing loss from average cost forces a fresh fundamental and valuation review. A 12% closing loss remains a default full exit. An exception requires new public evidence, a score of at least 75, and a written decision before the next order.
4. **Long-trend warning:** two consecutive closes below the 200-day average with negative six-month relative return versus SPY force a re-underwrite. The 50-day average is an alert, not an automatic sell rule. Price alone cannot overrule intact or broken primary evidence.
5. **Time and opportunity cost:** after twelve weeks, a position that has lagged SPY and produced no positive thesis milestone is sold or reduced. At weekly review, replace a holding only when a candidate scores at least ten points higher and offers better net asymmetry after tax and friction.
6. **Valuation or concentration:** trim when price exceeds the documented bull case without a corresponding evidence upgrade, when a position exceeds 30%, when a sector exceeds 45%, or when one economic driver exceeds 40% through appreciation.

There is no automatic profit target. Before earnings, reassess event risk three trading days in advance; a position above 20% should normally be reduced to 15–20% unless the log supports carrying it.

## Transition from Version 1.0

This amendment is prospective and cannot erase a decision signal that became valid under Version 1.0 before publication. Any such order must still be reconciled and revalidated against current broker and public evidence before submission.

Existing holdings receive a Forward Inflection transition review at the first complete broker-accessible session, with the full portfolio completed no later than five trading sessions after access is restored:

- **75 or above:** eligible to hold or add, subject to valuation and limits;
- **60–74:** no additions; write one dated, 30-day evidence milestone; sell if it is missed; and
- **below 60 or no credible structural role:** orderly exit at the next verified liquid opportunity.

A policy change alone is not a broker-verified trade instruction. Account state, settlement, restrictions, open orders, positions, quotes, and previews remain authoritative.

## Cadence and falsifiability

- **Daily:** reconcile the broker, inspect holdings for thesis changes, enforce exits, and research only genuinely actionable evidence.
- **Weekend:** update leading indicators, milestone status, earnings dates, valuation ranges, economic-driver exposure, and scores.
- **Quarterly:** rebuild the transition map from primary sources. Record which predicted bottlenecks became revenue, which did not, and why.
- **Monthly:** review attribution, turnover, drawdown, hit rate for leading indicators, and the time from thesis formation to estimate or revenue change.

Track every thesis forecast in the log as correct, early, wrong, or unresolved. A good stock return from a wrong causal thesis is luck. A correct trend bought at a price that loses money is still a failed investment decision.

Performance measurement remains unchanged:

```text
account return = current NAV / inception NAV - 1
SPY total return = current adjusted SPY value / inception adjusted SPY value - 1
active return = account return - SPY total return
```

Use broker NAV after orders and settlement are reconciled. Report account and SPY returns, drawdowns, turnover, win rate, average winner and loser, attribution, and forecast hit rate without selecting a convenient subperiod.
